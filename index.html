2. Reading: The Weekend Barbecue

Read the story carefully and answer the 5 questions below.

Every Sunday, Alex's family gathers for a barbecue at his grandparents' house. His grandfather, Arthur, always prepares the grill early in the morning. His aunt, Sarah, usually brings delicious ice cream for dessert, while his uncle, David, often plays guitar and sings with everyone. Alex's mother, Laura, is a nurse, so she is sometimes tired on weekends, but she never misses this family reunion because she loves spending quality time with her brother and cousins.
1. How often does Arthur prepare the grill?
2. What does Aunt Sarah usually bring for dessert?
3. What instrument does Uncle David often play?
4. Why is Alex's mother sometimes tired on weekends?
5. How often does Laura miss the family reunion?
